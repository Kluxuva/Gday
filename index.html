Every version of you — the sleepy one, the sulky-over-cheesecake one, the one who winks when she's being cheeky, the one who falls asleep mid-sentence on a call — is my favourite person to know. The distance is the hard part; I miss reaching for your hand without thinking, the ordinary kind of close. But some days feel full anyway, just quieter — and every mile is only ever temporary. Happy Girlfriend's Day, love. Here's a little garden of us. Every flower on this page is here because it reminded me of you, and we'll close this distance and stand together again, like we were never apart.
